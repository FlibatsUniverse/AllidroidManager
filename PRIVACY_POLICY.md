# Privacy Policy

Allidroid Manager does not collect, store on any server, or share your personal data. It has no accounts, analytics or advertising.

The app makes network requests only to do its job:

- GitHub, to check for manager updates, load recent commits, and list contributors.
- The Discord version tracker you pick in Advanced settings, to find the latest Discord version and download it.
- GitHub, to download Allidroid when you install or update it.

Install logs are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete them from the Logs screen. If you share a log, it goes wherever you choose to send it.

Allidroid Manager installs and patches apps on your device. Allidroid and Discord follow their own privacy policies, including [Discord's](https://discord.com/privacy/).
